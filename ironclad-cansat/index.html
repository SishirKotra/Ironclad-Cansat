One packet per second, as the ground laptop would log it. Baro altitude is what the firmware works out from pressure using the standard formula. It differs a little from the true height because Hyderabad air is warmer than the standard atmosphere.

      

      

        
What this model leaves out

        
    
          
  • The CanSat is treated as a point. Swinging under the parachute and spinning are not modelled. The 3D view tilts the CanSat along the airflow only to show direction.
    • 
          
  • Opening is a straight ramp. The canopy area grows evenly over the opening time. Real canopies can overshoot, so treat the peak load as a rough guide.
    • 
          
  • Drag coefficients are estimates. 0.8 for the flat canopy (ESA T10) and 0.8 for the can. Drop tests will give the real value.
    • 
          
  • Steady wind. No gusts or thermals. Wind grows with height using the standard 1/7 power law.
    • 
          
  • Gravity is constant (9.81 m/s²). Over 500 m it changes by less than 0.02%.
